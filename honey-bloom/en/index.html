Honey Bloom

Mobile puzzle game

Honey Bloom is a mobile puzzle game. You pick the front flower from a flower lane below the board. Worker bees carry its nectar to the honeycomb cells of the same color. You clear the level when you fill all target cells.

Features

  • Items: Recall Signal undoes the flower you just picked. Petal Perch adds one more leaf seat. Queen Call fills the color you pick at once.
  • Weekly league: You compete for one week against up to 30 players in your group.
  • Lives: You use one life when you fail a level. You can have up to 5 lives. One life refills every 20 minutes.
