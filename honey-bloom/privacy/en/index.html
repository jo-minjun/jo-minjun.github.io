Honey Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 (previous version: October 24, 2026)

Honey Bloom (the "App") is a mobile puzzle game made by independent developer Minjun Jo (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App keeps, where, and how it is used.

1. Data stored only on your device

The App does not ask you to sign up or log in. The data below stays on your device and is never sent to the Developer's servers.

  • Game progress and in-game currency
  • Settings
  • Ad reward records (ad attempt IDs and receipt IDs)

This data is deleted when you delete the App or use Settings > Delete account in the App (see section 4).

2. League (online rankings)

In the league you compete for one week against up to 30 players in the same group. When you clear a level and earn league points, the App creates an anonymous account and joins the league automatically. The App then sends the data below to the Developer's league server.

DataPurpose
Anonymous account ID (a random identifier created by the App)Telling players apart, group assignment
Nickname, chosen profile pictureShowing the leaderboard
Season, tier, total scoreRanking, promotion and demotion
  • Other players in your group can see your nickname, profile picture and score on the leaderboard. Do not use a nickname that identifies you, such as your real name or contact details.
  • The App does not send your email, phone number, advertising identifier or location to the league server.
  • When the App contacts the server, connection records such as your IP address may appear in the hosting provider's logs.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob.

  • Rewarded ads: you watch them only when you choose. They are used for an extra life, a doubled clear reward, boost time, continuing after a failure, and the video rewards on the home screen (petals, boost time and unlimited hearts).
  • Interstitial ads: they appear some of the time when you leave the result screen of a failed round. They give no reward.

To request and show ads, Google may collect and process the data below.

  • Device advertising identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and the iOS IDFA. On iOS the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device and operating-system information, app usage data, ad interaction data, diagnostic data

Google processes this data under its own policies. The Developer does not receive or store it. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Retention and deletion

The Developer keeps league records while the league service runs. League records are your anonymous account ID, nickname, profile picture, and season, tier and score. They are kept so that past seasons can be shown.

You can delete your account yourself in the App under Settings > Delete account. Deletion happens in this order.

  1. The server deletes, without delay, your league records for every season, your nickname, your profile picture and your anonymous (authentication) account.
  2. The App deletes the game progress, petals and items on your device.

Deleted data cannot be recovered.

  • If the App cannot confirm that the deletion on the server finished, for example because of a connection problem, it does not delete the data on your device. Some of your server records may already be deleted. Check your connection and try again.
  • If the App cannot verify your account, the server records cannot be deleted. This can happen, for example, when the league sign-in data on your device is damaged or the server refuses to confirm your identity. In that case only the data on your device is deleted, and your league records, nickname and profile picture remain on the server. Send the nickname you saw in the league and when you played to the contact in section 10, and we will delete them once we have found them.
  • When you ask for deletion by email or the league service ends, the Developer deletes the records from the server database without delay. Deleted records cannot be recovered.
  • Data on your device is deleted when you delete the App or delete your account.

6. Notifications

The App schedules two kinds of notifications on your device.

NotificationWhen
Life refillWhen you have no lives left, at the time your next life refills
Video rewardsAfter you have collected all the video rewards on the home screen, when their rest time ends

The App sends nothing to a server for these notifications. You can turn them off in device settings.

7. Consent and choices (including personalized ads)

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent screen on first launch. You can change your choice anytime in the App under Settings > Privacy settings.
  • On iOS the App asks whether you allow tracking. You can play and earn ad rewards the same way either way. Without permission you see non-personalized ads. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • You can delete your league records yourself in the App under Settings > Delete account (see section 4).
  • You can also ask by email to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your league records. Send the nickname shown in the league and when you played to the contact in section 10. The Developer will handle the request within 10 days and tell you the result.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Changes

If the information the App processes changes, the Developer updates this page and its effective date.
