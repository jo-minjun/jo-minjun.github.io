Honey Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 (previous version: November 1, 2026)

Honey Bloom (the "App") is a mobile puzzle game by the independent developer Jo Minjun (the "Developer"). This policy tells you which data the App uses, where the App keeps it, and how the App uses it.

1. Data stored on your device

The App keeps the data below on your device.

  • Game progress and in-game currency
  • Settings
  • Ad reward records (ad attempt IDs and receipt IDs)
  • Currency records that the App has not sent to the server yet (see section 2)

The vibration and reduce motion settings, the ad attempt records, and the reward records of life ads and continue-after-failure ads stay only on your device. The rewards of boost ads, video reward ads and doubled clear reward ads are sent to the currency ledger in section 2, with the ad receipt ID as the record ID. When you delete the App, the data on your device is deleted. The server data in section 2 stays when you delete the App, so you can get your progress and currency back when you install the App again.

2. Account and data stored on the server (Google Firebase)

The App has no sign-up or login screen. When the App first connects to the internet, it creates an anonymous account automatically. If your device is signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Play Games (Android), the App links that game account to the anonymous account without showing a screen. Because of this, you keep the same account with the same game account when you install the App again or change devices. The App stores the data below, linked to the account, on the Developer's server (Google Firebase).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two types of ads through Google AdMob.

  • Rewarded ads: You watch these ads only when you choose to. The App uses them for a life refill, a doubled clear reward, boost time, continuing after a failure, and the video rewards on the home screen (petals, boost time and unlimited lives).
  • Interstitial ads: When you leave the result screen of a round (failed or completed), the App sometimes shows this type of ad, based on chance and conditions. These ads give no reward.

To request and show ads, Google can collect and process the data below.

  • Device advertising identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and the iOS IDFA. On iOS, Google uses the IDFA only if you allow tracking.
  • Your IP address, and the approximate location that Google can get from it
  • Device and operating system data, app usage data, ad interaction data and diagnostic data

Google processes this data under its own policies. The Developer does not receive or keep this data. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

The Developer keeps the account data, progress backup, currency ledger and league records in section 2 while your account exists. League records are kept after a season ends, to show the rankings of past seasons.

However, an account that is not linked to a game account is deleted within 1 week after it has not been used for 1 year. The server then deletes the same data in the same sequence as Reset all data below, and keeps purchase records as pseudonymous records as described below. An account linked to a Game Center or Play Games account is not deleted, even when it is not used. A sign-in (authentication account) with no data stored on the server is not deleted either. Such a sign-in holds no information other than the anonymous account ID.

You can delete your account and its data yourself in the App under Settings > Reset all data. The deletion occurs in this sequence:

  1. The server marks the account as deleted. After this, the server refuses all writes from this account.
  2. The server moves your purchase records to pseudonymous records that are no longer linked to your account (see below).
  3. The server deletes, without delay, your progress backup, your currency ledger (balances and records), your short account ID, your league records for all seasons, your nickname, your profile picture, the transfer tickets this account created or redeemed, and your consent records for refund review data. Group records of a season that has ended but is not settled yet are deleted when that season is settled, so that the results of the other players in the group do not change.
  4. The server deletes your anonymous (authentication) account. The link to your Game Center or Play Games account is removed too.
  5. The App deletes the game progress, petals and items on your device.

You cannot recover deleted data. Petals and items do not come back.

  • Purchase records: the server keeps only the transaction ID, product, amount and currency, purchase time, platform, transaction environment, refund status and transaction events (event ID, type, time), and changes them into pseudonymous records that do not identify your account. The server keeps these records for 5 years from the date of purchase, and deletes them within 1 week after the 5 years end. Purchase records that are already more than 5 years old when the account is deleted are deleted, not moved to pseudonymous records. This is to keep transaction records as the applicable laws require. If a payment of a deleted account is refunded later, the server only marks the refund on the pseudonymous record. If another account that restored the same permanent product remains, that purchase record is not moved to pseudonymous records and stays with the remaining account.
  • Deleted-account mark: the server keeps the anonymous account ID of the deleted account, the time of deletion and the reason (account deletion or account switch). This prevents server records from being created again while the deleted account's sign-in is still valid for a short time. This mark holds none of the other data in section 2. The server deletes this mark automatically 30 days after the deletion.
  • Account switch: if the game account that the App tries to link to a new anonymous account is already linked to an earlier account, the App switches to the earlier account. The server then deletes the new account's data and authentication account in the same way. The new account's purchase records are not made pseudonymous and go to the earlier account. The buying account and the list of accounts that can use a permanent product also change to the earlier account, and the server records an owner change event for each changed purchase. Petals that the new account got through purchases are moved to the earlier account. The one-time transfer ticket used for the switch expires after 10 minutes. The server deletes the ticket record (the anonymous account IDs of the two accounts and the times) automatically 30 days after it was issued, or earlier when you delete the earlier account that the switch kept.
  • If there is no internet connection, if a server error occurs, or if the App cannot confirm the server deletion within 20 seconds, the App does not delete the data on your device and tells you that the deletion failed. Some of your server records can already be deleted. Check your connection and try again. When you try again, an account that is already deleted counts as deleted.
  • If you only played without an internet connection and no server account was created, the App deletes only the data on your device. The server has no data to delete.
  • When you ask for deletion by email, the Developer deletes the account's data in the same way.
  • When you only delete the App, only the data on your device is deleted, and the server data stays.

Data sent to Firebase for analytics and error reporting (section 4) is not linked to your account, so Reset all data in the App does not delete it. This data is kept for the periods below and then deleted.

  • Analytics event records: 2 months. User-level analytics records: 14 months after the last activity
  • Crash and error records: 90 days

The App schedules two types of notifications on your device.

NotificationWhen
Life refillWhen you have no lives left, at the time your next life refills
Video rewardsAfter you collect all the video rewards on the home screen, when their rest time ends

The App does not send data to a server for these notifications. You can turn off notifications in your device settings.

8. Consent and choices (including personalized ads)

  • When users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland start the App for the first time, the App shows Google's consent screen. This choice applies to both ads and Firebase analytics. You can change your consent at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the App asks if you allow tracking. If you do not allow tracking, you can still play and get ad rewards in the same way. If you do not allow tracking, you see non-personalized ads. You can change this choice in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • You can delete your account and server data yourself in the App under Settings > Reset all data (see section 5).
  • You can also ask by email to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of the data in section 2. Send the short account ID shown at the bottom of Settings to the contact in section 11. The Developer checks the request, completes it within 10 days and tells you the result.

9. Children

The App is not for children under 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

If the data that the App processes changes, the Developer updates this page and its effective date.
