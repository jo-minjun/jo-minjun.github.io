Honey Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 (previous version: November 1, 2026)

Honey Bloom (the "App") is a mobile puzzle game by the independent developer Jo Minjun (the "Developer"). This policy tells you which data the App uses, where the App keeps it, and how the App uses it.

1. Data stored only on your device

The App does not ask you to sign up or log in. The App keeps the data below only on your device. The App does not send this data to the Developer's servers.

  • Game progress and in-game currency
  • Settings
  • Ad reward records (ad attempt IDs and receipt IDs)

When you delete the App or use Settings > Reset all data in the App, this data is deleted too (see section 5).

2. League (online rankings)

In the league, you compete for one week against up to 30 players in the same group. When you clear a level and get league points, the App creates an anonymous account and joins the league automatically. The App then sends the data below to the Developer's league server.

DataPurpose
Anonymous account ID (a random identifier that the App creates)To identify each player, to assign groups
Nickname, chosen profile pictureTo show the leaderboard
Season, tier, total scoreTo calculate rankings, promotion and demotion
  • Other players in your group can see your nickname, profile picture and score on the leaderboard. Do not use a nickname that identifies you, such as your real name or contact details.
  • The App does not send your email address, phone number, advertising identifier or location to the league server.
  • When the App sends a request to the server, the hosting provider's logs can record connection data, such as your IP address.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two types of ads through Google AdMob.

  • Rewarded ads: You watch these ads only when you choose to. The App uses them for a life refill, a doubled clear reward, boost time, continuing after a failure, and the video rewards on the home screen (petals, boost time and unlimited lives).
  • Interstitial ads: When you leave the result screen of a round (failed or completed), the App sometimes shows this type of ad, based on chance and conditions. These ads give no reward.

To request and show ads, Google can collect and process the data below.

  • Device advertising identifiers: the Android Advertising ID and the iOS IDFA. On iOS, Google uses the IDFA only if you allow tracking.
  • Your IP address, and the approximate location that Google can get from it
  • Device and operating system data, app usage data, ad interaction data and diagnostic data

Google processes this data under its own policies. The Developer does not receive or keep this data. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. App analytics and error reporting (Google Firebase)

Copies of the App installed from the stores or from test distribution send the data below to Google Firebase. The Developer uses this data to tune game difficulty, fix errors and adjust how often ads appear.

DataPurpose
App instance ID (a random identifier created for each installation), and device data such as device model, operating system and app versionTo understand the number of users and their environments
Play records: level start, failure and completion with the level number, item use, petal spending, ad rewards received, interstitial ads shownTo find hard levels and where players stop, and to adjust ad frequency
Crash and error records (the code location of the error), and the type of a save failureTo fix errors
  • When the App starts, it gets the interstitial ad conditions from Firebase. This request also uses the app instance ID.
  • The App does not send your nickname, league account ID, email address or game save contents to Firebase. The App does not set a user ID in Firebase.
  • For users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the choice on the consent screen also applies to Firebase analytics (see section 8). If you do not consent, Google consent mode treats analytics storage as denied.

Google processes this data under the Firebase terms and Google's policies. For more information, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Retention and deletion

The Developer keeps league records while the league service operates. League records are your anonymous account ID, nickname, profile picture, season, tier and score. The Developer keeps them to show the rankings of past seasons.

You can delete your anonymous account and all your data yourself in the App under Settings > Reset all data. The deletion occurs in this sequence:

  1. The server deletes, without delay, your league records for all seasons, your nickname, your profile picture and your anonymous (authentication) account.
  2. The App deletes the game progress, petals and items on your device.

You cannot recover deleted data.

Data sent to Firebase is kept for the periods below and then deleted. Reset all data in the App does not delete data that was already sent to Firebase.

  • Analytics event records: 2 months. User-level analytics records: 14 months after the last activity
  • Crash and error records: 90 days
  • If the App cannot confirm that the server deletion is complete (for example, because of a connection problem), the App does not delete the data on your device. Some of your server records can already be deleted. Check your connection and try again.
  • If the App cannot verify your account, the App cannot delete your server records. For example, this occurs when the league sign-in data on your device is damaged, or when the server rejects the identity check. In this case, the App deletes only the data on your device. Your league records, nickname and profile picture stay on the server. Send the nickname that you saw in the league and when you played to the contact in section 11. The Developer finds the records and deletes them.
  • When you ask for deletion by email, or when the Developer ends the league service, the Developer deletes the records from the server database without delay. You cannot recover deleted records.
  • When you delete the App or your account, the data on your device is deleted too.

7. Notifications

The App schedules two types of notifications on your device.

NotificationWhen
Life refillWhen you have no lives left, at the time your next life refills
Video rewardsAfter you collect all the video rewards on the home screen, when their rest time ends

The App does not send data to a server for these notifications. You can turn off notifications in your device settings.

8. Consent and choices (including personalized ads)

  • When users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland start the App for the first time, the App shows Google's consent screen. This choice applies to both ads and Firebase analytics. You can change your consent at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy settings.
  • On iOS, the App asks if you allow tracking. If you do not allow tracking, you can still play and get ad rewards in the same way. If you do not allow tracking, you see non-personalized ads. You can change this choice in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings.
  • You can delete your league records yourself in the App under Settings > Reset all data (see section 5).
  • You can also ask by email to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your league records. Send the nickname shown in the league and when you played to the contact in section 11. The Developer checks the request, completes it within 10 days and tells you the result.

9. Children

The App is not for children under 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

If the data that the App processes changes, the Developer updates this page and its effective date.
