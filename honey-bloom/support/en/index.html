Honey Bloom Support

Honey Bloom is a honeycomb puzzle game by the independent developer Jo Minjun. If you have a problem or a question, send us an email.

Contact: alswns9288@gmail.com

In your email, include your device model, OS version, app version, and the level and situation where the problem occurred. This information helps us find the problem faster. The app version is in Settings.

FAQ

What happens to my progress if I delete the app?

The app backs up your game progress to your account and keeps your petals, items, boost time and unlimited lives on the server. If you link Game Center (iOS) or Play Games (Android) and install the app again with the same game account, your progress and currency come back. If you delete the app without linking a game account, you cannot get your progress and currency back. You can see the link status in the Connect Game Center (Android: Connect Play Games) row in Settings.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Yes, between devices that use the same Game Center or Play Games account. Start the app on the new device while it is signed in to the same game account. The app finds your earlier account and restores your progress and currency. You cannot move progress between iOS and Android.

I watched an ad but did not get the reward.

Wait a moment, then tap the same ad button again. The app checks the result of your earlier ad without showing a new ad. If the app cannot confirm that you watched the whole ad, there is no reward.

We do not add missed ad rewards for you. If the problem keeps happening, email us your device model, app version, and the time you watched the ad. We will find the cause and fix the app.

How do I report an objectionable nickname?

Send the nickname, the season, and what happened to the email above. We check each report and act on it within 24 hours. The nickname rules are in section 4 of the Terms of Use.

How do I delete all my data and league records?

In the app, go to Settings > Reset all data. This deletes your progress backup, your petal and item balances and records, your league records, nickname, profile picture and anonymous account on the server. It also deletes the game progress, petals and items on your device. You cannot undo this. As the law requires, purchase records are kept for 5 years as pseudonymous records that are no longer linked to your account.

If the app cannot confirm that the server deletion is complete (for example, because of a connection problem), the app does not delete the data on your device. Some of your server records can already be deleted. Check your connection and try again.

If you cannot delete your data in the app, send us the account ID shown at the bottom of Settings. We check it and delete the data. For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
