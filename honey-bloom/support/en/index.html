Honey Bloom Support

Honey Bloom is a honeycomb puzzle game by the independent developer Jo Minjun. If you have a problem or a question, send us an email.

Contact: alswns9288@gmail.com

In your email, include your device model, OS version, app version, and the level and situation where the problem occurred. This information helps us find the problem faster. The app version is in Settings.

FAQ

What happens to my progress if I delete the app?

The app keeps game progress and currency only on your device. When you delete the app, this data is deleted too. You cannot restore it.

Can I move my progress to another device?

No. The app does not support this yet.

I watched an ad but did not get the reward.

If the reward did not arrive after you watched the whole ad, open the app again. If the reward still does not arrive, send us an email with the time when you watched the ad.

How do I report an objectionable nickname?

Send the nickname, the season, and what happened to the email above. We check each report and act on it within 24 hours. The nickname rules are in section 4 of the Terms of Use.

How do I delete all my data and league records?

In the app, go to Settings > Reset all data. This deletes your league records, nickname, profile picture and anonymous account on the server. It also deletes the game progress, petals and items on your device. You cannot undo this.

If the app cannot confirm that the server deletion is complete (for example, because of a connection problem), the app does not delete the data on your device. Some of your server records can already be deleted. Check your connection and try again.

In rare cases, the app cannot verify your account. Then the app deletes only the data on your device. Your league records, nickname and profile picture stay on the server. Send us the nickname that you saw in the league and when you played. We find the records and delete them. For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
